1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW AND MSME DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MSME Loans are credit facilities extended to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises as defined under the MSME Development Act 2006 (amended 2020) and the revised RBI classification:

Classification (as per revised definition effective July 2020):

Enterprise Type | Investment in Plant & Machinery / Equipment | Annual Turnover
----------------|----------------------------------------------|----------------
Micro           | Up to ₹1 crore                              | Up to ₹5 crore
Small           | Up to ₹10 crore                             | Up to ₹50 crore
Medium          | Up to ₹50 crore                             | Up to ₹250 crore

Eligible business types:
- Manufacturing enterprises (producing goods)
- Service enterprises (professional services, IT, healthcare, hospitality)
- Trading enterprises (wholesale/retail, import-export)

Eligible loan purposes:
- Working capital: inventory purchase, raw material procurement, trade payables
- Term loan: machinery purchase, equipment upgrade, technology investment
- Business expansion: additional premises, capacity addition
- Receivables financing: invoice discounting for B2B businesses

Not covered under MSME Loan:
- Startup businesses with less than 1 year of operations (see Startup credit products)
- Agricultural activity / farm mechanisation (separate Agri loan products apply)
- Personal expenses of the proprietor — must not be co-mingled

2. UDYAM REGISTRATION — MANDATORY REQUIREMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All MSME borrowers must have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) issued by the Ministry of MSME via the Udyam Registration Portal (udyamregistration.gov.in).

Registration is free, fully online, and based on PAN and Aadhaar. It replaces the older Udyog Aadhaar (UAM) registration.

Udyam Registration is required for:
- Classification under the MSME definition (determines applicable product and scheme)
- Access to government-backed schemes (CGTMSE, ECLGS)
- Priority Sector Lending (PSL) benefits
- Subsidised interest rate schemes announced by Central/State governments

Applications without a valid Udyam Registration Certificate will be processed as general business loans, not MSME loans, and will not attract CGTMSE coverage or PSL benefits.

3. INTEREST RATES (INDICATIVE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MSME loan rates are linked to the bank's EBLR (External Benchmark Lending Rate), which is repo-rate linked, and reset quarterly.

Indicative rate range (as of June 2026):

Loan Category                            | Interest Rate
-----------------------------------------|----------------
Mudra Loans (up to ₹10 lakh)             | 8.50% – 10.50%
Micro enterprise (CGTMSE covered)        | 9.00% – 11.00%
Small enterprise (secured)               | 9.50% – 12.00%
Medium enterprise (secured)              | 10.00% – 13.00%
Unsecured MSME (small ticket)            | 12.00% – 16.00%
ECLGS (Emergency Credit Line)            | Capped at 9.25% (scheme-specific)

Government-backed schemes:
- CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises): Collateral-free loans up to ₹1 crore with credit guarantee cover. Interest rate loading of 0.25–1.5% for guarantee premium.
- PM SVANidhi: Micro-credit for street vendors up to ₹50,000 at subsidised rates.
- MUDRA Yojana (Shishu / Kishore / Tarun): ₹50,000 to ₹10 lakh without collateral for micro enterprises.

4. LOAN AMOUNT AND TENURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Loan amount ranges:
- MUDRA / Micro: ₹50,000 to ₹10 lakh
- Small enterprise term loan: ₹10 lakh to ₹2 crore
- Medium enterprise term loan: ₹2 crore to ₹10 crore
- Working capital (CC/OD limit): Based on assessed annual turnover (typically 20–25% of annual turnover)

Tenure:
- Working capital (cash credit / overdraft): Reviewed annually; renewed subject to satisfactory conduct
- Equipment / machinery term loans: 3 to 7 years
- Infrastructure / premises loans: 5 to 15 years
- Emergency Credit Line (ECLGS): Up to 5 years including moratorium

Collateral requirement:
- Loans up to ₹1 crore: Collateral-free under CGTMSE scheme (guarantee premium charged to borrower)
- Loans ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore: Partial collateral or enhanced documentation required
- Loans above ₹5 crore: Full collateral (property mortgage, fixed deposits, plant hypothecation)

5. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Business Vintage
- Minimum: 3 years of continuous operations in the same line of business
- Startups (less than 3 years): Evaluated separately under Startup credit — not under standard MSME loan

5.2 Turnover and Income
- Minimum annual turnover: ₹10 lakh (for small ticket loans); higher thresholds for larger loans
- ITR requirement: Minimum 2 years of filed and assessed Income Tax Returns (proprietorship / partnership / company)
- Profitability: Business must show net profit in at least 1 of the last 2 years
- For self-employed professionals: Net annual income (after tax) of at least ₹3 lakh

5.3 Age of Applicant / Proprietor
- Minimum: 21 years
- Maximum: 65 years at loan maturity

5.4 Credit Score
- Minimum CIBIL score (individual): 700 (proprietors and partners); 750 preferred
- CIBIL MSME Rank (CMR): CMR 1–6 preferred; CMR 7 or above requires additional justification
- No existing NPA or write-off on business or personal credit bureau report
- No overdues more than 90 days in the last 12 months

5.5 GST Compliance
- GST registration mandatory for businesses with turnover above the applicable threshold (₹20 lakh for services, ₹40 lakh for goods)
- GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) for the last 12 months reviewed for turnover verification
- Mismatch between ITR income and GST-declared turnover is a red flag and requires explanation

5.6 FOIR (for term loans with fixed EMI)
- Maximum FOIR: 65% of net business income (more lenient than personal loans given irregular cash flows)
- For working capital (CC/OD): Evaluated on DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) — minimum DSCR of 1.25 required

6. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 KYC — Business Entity
- Udyam Registration Certificate (mandatory)
- GST Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Business registration document:
  • Sole Proprietorship: Shop & Establishment Act certificate, or Trade Licence
  • Partnership: Registered Partnership Deed
  • LLP: LLP Agreement and Certificate of Incorporation
  • Pvt Ltd / Public Ltd: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA & AOA

6.2 KYC — Individual (Proprietor / Partners / Directors)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- PAN Card (mandatory)
- Address proof (Aadhaar / Passport / Utility bill)
- Photograph

6.3 Financial Documents
- ITR with computation of income for last 2 financial years (CA-certified)
- Audited balance sheet and P&L account for last 2 years (for loans above ₹10 lakh)
- For smaller loans: Self-certified balance sheet acceptable (subject to credit policy)
- Bank statement: Last 12 months — all business accounts (primary operating account mandatory)
- GST returns: GSTR-3B for last 12 months

6.4 Business Proof and Purpose Documents
- For machinery/equipment loan: Quotation from supplier (on letterhead), equipment details
- For premises: Agreement to lease or sale deed / property documents
- For working capital: Latest stock statement, trade receivables list, supplier invoice samples
- For CGTMSE cover: Self-declaration of MSME status (Udyam registration sufficient)

6.5 Existing Loan Details (if any)
- Sanction letters and latest repayment statements for all existing business and personal loans

7. PROCESSING FEES AND CHARGES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Processing fee: 0.50% to 1.00% of sanctioned loan amount + GST (minimum ₹2,500; waived for MUDRA loans under Shishu category)
CGTMSE annual guarantee fee: 0.37% to 1.35% of guaranteed amount (paid annually; can be built into loan or paid separately)
Documentation and legal charges: ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 (depending on loan complexity)
Prepayment:
  - Floating rate: No prepayment penalty (RBI guideline)
  - Fixed rate term loans: 2% on prepaid principal
Renewal fee for CC/OD limits: 0.25% to 0.50% of limit + GST (charged annually at renewal)

8. TYPICAL APPRAISAL AND APPROVAL PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1 — Pre-qualification: Copilot collects business profile (type, vintage, turnover, purpose). Indicative loan band communicated.
Step 2 — Document collection: Full document list shared. Applicant submits all required documents.
Step 3 — Credit appraisal: Credit team analyses financials, verifies GST/ITR consistency, runs bureau check.
Step 4 — Field investigation: For loans above ₹50 lakh, bank may conduct site visit to business premises.
Step 5 — Sanction: Credit committee approves. Sanction letter issued with facility details, covenants, and conditions.
Step 6 — Documentation and disbursement: Legal documentation executed. For CC/OD, limit activated. For term loans, funds released to vendor or business account.

Turnaround time:
- Mudra / micro loans (up to ₹10 lakh): 3–7 working days (59-minute in-principle available on government PSB portal)
- Small enterprise (₹10 lakh to ₹2 crore): 7–15 working days
- Medium enterprise: 15–30 working days (field investigation required)

9. COMMON REJECTION REASONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Business vintage less than 3 years
- Missing or outdated Udyam Registration Certificate
- ITR not filed for 2 consecutive years or income not matching bank turnover
- CIBIL score below 700 (individual or business)
- Significant mismatch between GST-declared turnover and ITR income (triggers fraud suspicion)
- Business showing losses in both assessment years
- Existing NPA on business or personal credit
- Incomplete documentation — missing bank statements or financials
