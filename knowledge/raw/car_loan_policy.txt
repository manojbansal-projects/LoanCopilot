1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW AND HARD SCOPE BOUNDARIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A New Car Loan is a secured credit facility for the purchase of a brand-new passenger car from an authorized dealership. The vehicle being purchased serves as collateral through hypothecation in favour of the bank.

THIS PRODUCT COVERS:
- Brand-new passenger cars (hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, MUVs) purchased from manufacturer-authorized dealerships
- On-road price financing including ex-showroom price, road tax, registration charges, and insurance (first year)
- Both salaried and self-employed individuals

THIS PRODUCT DOES NOT COVER — AGENT MUST REDIRECT:
- Used, second-hand, or pre-owned vehicles of any age → Redirect: used car loan product (not in scope for this copilot)
- Two-wheelers: motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, e-bikes → Redirect: two-wheeler loan product (not in scope)
- Three-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws → Redirect: commercial vehicle loan (not in scope)
- Commercial vehicles: taxis, trucks, school buses, goods carriers → Redirect: commercial vehicle loan (not in scope)
- Refinancing or top-up on an existing car loan → Not available under this product
- Electric vehicles — covered under same product; mention FAME-II subsidy if applicable
- Dealer-linked subvented schemes with zero-interest offers → These are marketing arrangements between dealer and financier; bank's standard rates apply unless otherwise notified

2. INTEREST RATES (INDICATIVE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

New car loan rates are typically fixed for the loan tenure and are determined by credit profile and vehicle segment.

Indicative rates (as of June 2026):

Applicant Profile                          | Interest Rate
------------------------------------------|----------------
Salaried, CIBIL 760+                      | 8.75% – 9.25%
Salaried, CIBIL 700–759                   | 9.25% – 10.25%
Self-employed professional, CIBIL 750+    | 9.50% – 10.50%
Self-employed non-professional, CIBIL 700+| 10.50% – 12.50%
Existing bank customer (salary account)   | 0.25% concession on applicable slab

Reference rates: SBI new car loan starts at 8.95% p.a.; HDFC Bank starts at 8.75% p.a. for CIBIL 760+ salaried customers (FY 2026-27).

Note: Electric vehicle (EV) purchases may attract 0.25% rate concession under the bank's Green Mobility initiative, subject to the vehicle being BIS-certified and FAME-II registered.

3. LOAN AMOUNT AND LTV (LOAN-TO-VALUE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Loan amount range: ₹3 lakh to ₹20 lakh (standard retail segment)
For premium / luxury vehicles: up to ₹75 lakh subject to enhanced income verification and credit committee approval. This is outside the standard advisory scope of this copilot.

LTV (Loan-to-Value) ratio:
- Standard: Up to 85% of the on-road price
- High CIBIL (800+): Up to 90% of on-road price offered by select banks
- Minimum down payment by borrower: 10% to 15% of on-road price

Note: The on-road price includes ex-showroom price + road tax + registration + insurance (1st year) + accessories (if dealer-fitted). The bank finances the on-road price, not just the ex-showroom price.

Income cap: Maximum eligible loan = the lesser of (85% of on-road price) OR (10x net monthly income)
Example: Applicant earning ₹40,000/month is eligible for a maximum of ₹4 lakh on the income cap, regardless of the vehicle's on-road price.

4. TENURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Minimum tenure: 12 months
Maximum tenure: 84 months (7 years) for new cars

Recommended tenure guidance:
- Shorter tenure (12–36 months): Higher EMI, lower interest outgo, faster ownership
- Longer tenure (60–84 months): Lower EMI, significantly higher total interest paid
- Most customers choose 36–60 months for a balance of manageable EMI and reasonable interest cost

The copilot should always present EMI for at least 3 tenure options (e.g., 36 / 60 / 84 months) so the applicant can make an informed choice.

5. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Age
- Minimum: 21 years
- Maximum: 70 years at loan maturity (most banks allow up to 65 for self-employed)

5.2 Income — Salaried
- Minimum net annual income: ₹2.50 lakh (₹20,833/month)
- Employment: Permanent employee with at least 1 year of work experience
- Stable salary credit to bank account (verified via bank statement)

5.3 Income — Self-Employed
- Minimum net annual income: ₹3 lakh (as per ITR)
- Business vintage: Minimum 2 years
- ITR filing: At least 1 year of filed and assessed ITR required

5.4 Credit Score
- Minimum CIBIL score: 700
- Preferred: 750 and above for best rates
- No active NPA, write-off, or settlement in credit bureau report

5.5 FOIR
- Maximum 50% of net monthly income including the proposed car loan EMI
- All existing loan EMIs and credit card minimums are included

6. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Mandatory for ALL applicants
- Proforma Invoice (PI) from the authorized dealer: Shows vehicle make, model, variant, ex-showroom price, road tax, insurance, and total on-road price. THIS IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT DOCUMENT — loan cannot be processed without it.
- Aadhaar Card (KYC)
- PAN Card (mandatory)
- Address proof (Aadhaar or utility bill not older than 3 months)

6.2 Income Documents — Salaried
- Latest 3 months' salary slips
- Last 6 months' bank statement (salary account)
- Form 16 for last financial year (optional but preferred for higher loan amounts)

6.3 Income Documents — Self-Employed
- ITR with income computation for last 1–2 financial years
- Last 6 months' bank statement (business/savings account)
- Business registration proof (GST certificate / Shop Act)

6.4 No property documents required
A new car loan is secured against the vehicle itself (hypothecation). No property, fixed deposit, or other collateral is required. This distinguishes it from a Home Loan.

6.5 Post-disbursement documents (submitted within 90 days)
- RC (Registration Certificate) with bank's hypothecation endorsement
- Comprehensive insurance policy with bank named as hypothecatee

Note: These documents are submitted after purchase and registration. The bank may place a hold on future credit if these are not submitted within 90 days.

7. PROCESSING FEES AND CHARGES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Processing fee: 0.40% to 1.00% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹1,500; maximum ₹5,000)
Prepayment / foreclosure:
  - Within 6 months: Not permitted
  - 7–12 months: 5% of outstanding principal
  - After 12 months: 3% of outstanding principal
  - After 24 months: 2% of outstanding principal
Duplicate RC / NOC charges: ₹500 (for issuing No Objection Certificate on full repayment)
Late payment: 2% per month on overdue EMI amount

8. APPLICATION AND DISBURSEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1 — Pre-qualification: Copilot collects income, employment type, desired vehicle (on-road price), down payment ability. Provides indicative loan amount and EMI range.
Step 2 — Application: Customer visits branch or applies online. Submits proforma invoice and income documents.
Step 3 — Credit check and approval: Bank verifies documents, runs CIBIL, calculates FOIR. Approval typically in 1–3 working days for salaried applicants.
Step 4 — Loan agreement execution: Customer signs loan agreement and hypothecation deed (can be done at dealership in tie-up banks).
Step 5 — Disbursement: Funds transferred directly to the dealership's bank account. Customer pays the down payment directly to the dealer. Bank does not disburse to the customer's personal account.
Step 6 — Vehicle collection: Customer collects vehicle and registration documents from the dealer.

Typical turnaround: Same-day to 3 working days for salaried applicants with clean profiles.
