1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A Home Loan enables eligible borrowers to purchase a residential property, construct a house on a plot they own, or undertake major renovation of an existing residential property. It is a secured, long-tenure product where the underlying property serves as collateral through a registered mortgage.

Eligible purposes:
- Purchase of a new flat, apartment, or independent house from a builder or resale seller
- Construction of a house on a self-owned plot
- Purchase of a plot and simultaneous construction
- Home extension or major renovation (subject to sub-limits)

Not eligible under this product:
- Commercial property purchase
- Purchase of agricultural land
- Loan against an already owned property (see Loan Against Property — separate product)

2. INTEREST RATES (INDICATIVE — FLOATING)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Home loan interest rates are floating and linked to the bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), which is tied to RBI's repo rate. Rates reset quarterly.

Indicative rate range (as of June 2026):
- Salaried applicants (CIBIL 750+): 8.50% – 9.25% p.a.
- Salaried applicants (CIBIL 650–749): 9.25% – 9.75% p.a.
- Self-employed professionals (CIBIL 750+): 8.75% – 9.50% p.a.
- Self-employed non-professionals (CIBIL 700+): 9.50% – 10.25% p.a.

Special concessions:
- Women borrowers (sole or primary applicant): 0.05% reduction on applicable rate
- Existing salary account holders: 0.10% reduction (subject to approval)
- Green-rated buildings (BEE 4-star and above): 0.05% reduction

Note: Final rate is determined after credit appraisal and is not guaranteed at inquiry stage. The above figures are indicative ranges published for customer guidance.

3. LOAN AMOUNT AND LTV (LOAN-TO-VALUE) RATIO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The maximum loan amount is governed by two limits — LTV ratio and income-based eligibility — and the lower of the two applies.

LTV limits (as per RBI guidelines):
- Property value up to ₹30 lakh: maximum LTV 90% (borrower brings 10% margin)
- Property value ₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh: maximum LTV 80%
- Property value above ₹75 lakh: maximum LTV 75%

Income-based eligibility:
- Maximum eligible EMI: up to 50% of net monthly income (FOIR cap)
- As a thumb rule, a borrower earning ₹50,000 net/month is eligible for approximately ₹33–36 lakh over a 20-year tenure at prevailing rates

Minimum loan amount: ₹5 lakh
Maximum loan amount: ₹5 crore (higher amounts subject to special approval and enhanced documentation)

4. TENURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Minimum tenure: 5 years
Maximum tenure: 30 years (subject to age constraint below)

Age-tenure linkage:
- The loan must be fully repaid by the time the borrower turns 70 (salaried) or 75 (self-employed/business)
- Example: A 45-year-old salaried borrower can avail maximum 25-year tenure
- Example: A 35-year-old self-employed borrower can avail maximum 30-year tenure

Longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases total interest outgo. The copilot should illustrate this tradeoff when discussing tenure options with applicants.

5. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Age
- Minimum age: 21 years at loan origination
- Maximum age at loan maturity: 70 years (salaried), 75 years (self-employed)

5.2 Income — Salaried Applicants
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹25,000 (metro cities); ₹20,000 (Tier 2/3 cities)
- Employment type: Permanent employee of Government, PSU, listed company, or reputed private organisation
- Minimum work experience: 2 years total; 6 months in current job
- Acceptable income components: Basic salary, HRA, fixed allowances. Variable pay counted at 50% if declared in Form 16.

5.3 Income — Self-Employed Applicants
- Minimum annual net profit (after tax): ₹3 lakh (professionals); ₹4 lakh (non-professionals)
- Business vintage: Minimum 3 years in same line of business
- ITR filing: Minimum 2 years of filed and assessed ITRs required
- Acceptable income: Net profit from ITR + proportionate depreciation add-back (at 50%)

5.4 Credit Score (CIBIL / Equifax)
- Minimum acceptable score: 650
- Preferred score for best rates: 750 and above
- No active defaults, write-offs, or settlements in the last 3 years
- NTC (New to Credit) applicants evaluated on income strength and employment stability

5.5 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
- FOIR = (All existing loan EMIs + proposed new EMI) / Net Monthly Income
- Maximum FOIR allowed: 50% for incomes up to ₹75,000/month; 55% for incomes above ₹75,000/month
- All existing EMIs (car loan, personal loan, credit card minimum dues) are included in FOIR calculation

6. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Identity and Address Proof (any one each)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for KYC)
- PAN Card (mandatory for all applicants)
- Passport / Voter ID / Driving Licence (address proof)
- Utility bill not older than 3 months (address proof)

6.2 Income Documents — Salaried
- Latest 3 months' salary slips
- Form 16 for last 2 financial years
- 6 months' bank statement (salary account)
- Appointment letter or employment confirmation letter

6.3 Income Documents — Self-Employed
- ITR with computation of income for last 2 financial years (CA-certified)
- Audited balance sheet and profit & loss statement for last 2 years
- 12 months' bank statement (business + personal account)
- Business registration certificate (GST certificate / Shop Act / Partnership Deed / MOA-AOA)

6.4 Property Documents
- Agreement to Sale / Allotment Letter from builder
- Property title deed (in case of resale property)
- Approved building plan and layout plan
- Property tax receipts (for resale property)
- NOC from builder / society / housing board
- Encumbrance Certificate (for South India registrations)
- Sale Deed executed in favour of seller (resale)

6.5 Additional Documents (case-specific)
- Co-applicant KYC and income documents (if loan is joint)
- Existing property documents (if offering additional collateral)
- Bank statements of NRI income account (if NRI co-applicant)

7. PROCESSING FEES AND CHARGES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Processing fee: 0.25% to 0.50% of sanctioned loan amount (minimum ₹3,000; maximum ₹20,000) + applicable GST
Legal and technical charges: ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 (varies by property location and value)
Stamp duty on mortgage: As per respective state government regulations
Prepayment charges: Nil for floating rate loans (as per RBI directive); fixed rate loans may carry 2% on outstanding principal if prepaid from own funds
Late payment penalty: 2% per month on overdue EMI amount

8. TYPICAL APPLICATION JOURNEY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1 — Inquiry and pre-qualification: Customer provides income, purpose, and property details. Copilot provides indicative eligibility and EMI range.
Step 2 — Application submission: Customer submits completed application form with all mandatory documents.
Step 3 — Credit appraisal: Bank verifies income documents, pulls CIBIL report, and calculates FOIR.
Step 4 — Property legal and technical check: Bank-empanelled advocate verifies title; bank-empanelled valuer assesses market value.
Step 5 — Sanction letter: If approved, sanction letter issued with loan amount, rate, tenure, and conditions.
Step 6 — Disbursement: After property registration and mortgage creation, funds disbursed to builder/seller account. Construction loans disbursed in tranches.

Typical turnaround time: 7–15 working days from complete document submission to sanction.

9. COMMON REASONS FOR REJECTION OR REDUCTION IN LOAN AMOUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- CIBIL score below 650 or recent loan default/settlement
- FOIR exceeding 50–55% of net income
- Property in a non-approved location or disputed title
- Insufficient income documentation or income below minimum threshold
- Business vintage less than 3 years (self-employed)
- Property valuation lower than purchase consideration (bank funds basis valuation, not purchase price)
- Incomplete property documents or missing NOC from builder
